title=Quick Crafting
description=Scales down how long crafting takes.
description=
description=Profession recipes in Build 42 can eat minutes of real time. This mod runs hand crafting at a percentage of its normal duration: at the default 10%, a 40 second recipe takes 4 seconds.
description=
description=Recipes keep their [b]relative[/b] cost. A quick recipe stays quicker than an elaborate one, which is the point of scaling rather than clamping everything to the same value.
description=
description=[h1]Options[/h1]
description=Under Sandbox Options > Quick Crafting:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Scale crafting duration[/b] - on by default
description=[*][b]Crafting time (% of normal)[/b] - default 10, range 1 to 100. At 100 nothing changes.
description=[*][b]Hard ceiling (seconds)[/b] - optional, applied after the scaling. 0 disables it.
description=[/list]
description=Server admins can tune this freely: 25% with a 10 second ceiling keeps short and long recipes feeling different while guaranteeing nothing drags.
description=
description=[h1]Scope[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Only hand crafting is affected. Reading, foraging, digging, sawing and medical actions keep their vanilla timings.
description=[*]The scaling is applied after the game's unhappiness, drunkenness and pain multipliers, so those cannot undo the reduction.
description=[*]Craft benches and processors such as forges and kilns run their progress in Java, which Lua cannot reach, so those are not affected.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Notes[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Built for Build 42.20.
description=[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
description=[*]Tested in singleplayer. Multiplayer should work but has not been verified.
description=[/list]
